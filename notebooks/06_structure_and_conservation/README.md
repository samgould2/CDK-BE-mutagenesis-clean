# 06 — structure and conservation

`conservation.ipynb` builds the per-position conservation tables from the
ortholog and kinome alignments in `data/reference/conservation/`.

The KLIFS pocket analyses — resistance by pocket position, the cross-kinase
alignment behind figure 6 and supplementary 15 — live in
`cdkbe.source_data.klifs_msa` and `klifs_resistance`, and the structure
renders are made by `scripts/chimerax/`. `docs/METHODS.md` §3 covers both.
