Two independent signatures separate the control from all three variant lines:
* **Focal 9p21.3 loss** — MTAP, CDKN2A and the adjacent KLHL9 all drop ~20-40x in every variant line, while the flanking CDKN2B-AS1 and TUSC1 are unchanged. A clean focal deletion, not a transcriptional response.
* **Loss of the ER/luminal program** — ESR1, PGR, GREB1, TFF1, FOXA1, GATA3, CDH1 and EPCAM collapse to near zero and VIM rises, in all three variants.

Neither is a plausible consequence of editing CDK4. 25 genes are expressed in the control (>5 TPM) and silent in all three variants (<0.5 TPM) while **0** genes go the other way, so the variant lines look like a de-differentiated, 9p21-deleted derivative of the same parental stock rather than a different cell line. The practical consequence is the same either way: **variant-vs-CBE NT contrasts are confounded by cell-line background**, and that confounder is 79% of the variance in the dataset.

## 3. RB-E2F program

Note on direction: CDK4 phosphorylates RB, which releases E2F. Loss of CDK4 keeps RB active and therefore **lowers** E2F target transcription — the same direction palbociclib produces. An *elevated* E2F program is what RB1 or CDKN2A loss gives, not CDK4 loss. The data below move in the loss-of-function direction.

Mean z-score within the 9 variant samples (control excluded so the background cancels). z-scores are scale-free, so with triplicates this tight almost anything reaches significance — read the stars as "consistent across replicates", and take the effect size from the fold-change table in section 2. ## 4. Does D196N or E56K phenocopy the null?

Correlating each variant's log2FC-vs-CBE NT against W198\*'s over the 2179 W198\* DEGs gives r = D196N 0.94 (slope 0.85), E56K 0.79 (slope 0.48). **These numbers are not interpretable as phenocopy scores**: all three variants share the 9p21-deleted, ER-negative background and the control does not, so most of what is being correlated is that shared background rather than CDK4 genotype.

The within-background evidence instead says:
* W198\* vs the missense lines yields only 26 DEGs at |log2FC|>1 — CDK4 itself plus a residual set of epithelial genes (CDH1, CLDN3/4, RAB25, S100A9) that tracks E56K's slightly more luminal state.
* On the S-phase/E2F axis the three lines rank E56K > D196N > W198\*, with D196N roughly midway between the two on a log scale — consistent with D196N being a **partial** loss-of-function allele and E56K retaining function on this readout.
* D196N and W198\* are nearly superimposed on the variant-only PCA, but that axis is dominated by residual identity drift, so it should not be read as functional equivalence on its own.
